About us

We are Team 5104, a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team from Pacific Grove, California known as the BreakerBots. Coach Roby Hyde started our team in 2013 with the goal of providing a welcoming space for students who shared an interest in STEM. Since then, our team has grown to about 8 mentors and 35 active students, with over 1/3 of them women or non binary. Our focus has expanded beyond science as well; we now have students working on business, project management, communications, art and team spirit.

Although we meet year-round, our busiest time begins in January when we have just six weeks to design and build a robot that can compete in a specifically designed robot game. We typically enter two regional competitions in California each year, and we have qualified for the FIRST World Championships in Houston, Texas twice in the past five years. This year, we competed in, FIRST’s unique “varsity sport for the mind."

Nausicaa 2026 - Rebuilt

Nausicaa, our 2026 robot, highlights the efforts of our team in every subsystem. Our robot is capable of intaking from the ground and human player station, traversing under the trench and over the bump, passing fuel from the opposing alliance zone/neutral zone into the alliance zone, and scoring from anywhere in our alliance zone.

About FIRST

For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology (FIRST) is a non-profit organization founded by Dean Kamen in 1989 with the goal of inspiring young people to pursue an education in STEM-related fields. Through competition, cooperation, and gracious professionalism, FIRST reaches students throughout the United States and the world. You can find more information at firstinspires.org.
